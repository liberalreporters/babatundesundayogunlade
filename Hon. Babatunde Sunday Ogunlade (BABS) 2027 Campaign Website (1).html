Press Release

Hon. Babs Declares Intention for Oyo State House of Assembly 2027

October 2026 • Eruwa, Oyo State

In a momentous gathering of party stalwarts, youth leaders, and elders in Eruwa, Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade (BABS) officially declared his intention to contest for the Oyo State House of Assembly under the platform of the All Progressives Congress (APC) representing Ibarapa East Constituency for the 2027 general elections.

Highlighting his rich background as an M.Sc. holder in Accounting from the University of Lagos and Managing Director of Skylark Homes & Properties Ltd, Hon. Babs emphasized that his legislative agenda is anchored on financial prudence, infrastructural growth, youth empowerment, and robust community welfare.

"We have the vision to lead and the capacity to deliver. Ibarapa East deserves transparent, accountable, and vibrant representation," Hon. Babs stated to thunderous applause.

Community Outreach

Empowering Ibarapa Youths Through Digital & Vocational Skills

September 2026 • Ibarapa East

As part of his continuous commitment to grassroots human capital development, Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade sponsored a comprehensive youth skills acquisition workshop across Ibarapa East.

The program equipped dozens of young men and women with vocational training, digital literacy tools, and startup grants to foster economic self-reliance. Beneficiaries expressed profound gratitude for his unwavering dedication to the community's progress.
