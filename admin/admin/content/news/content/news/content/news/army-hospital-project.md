The All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for the Oyo State House of Assembly in Ibarapa East Constituency, Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade (BABS), has commended the Nigerian Army for its continued commitment to civil-military cooperation and community development, following the commissioning of the Chief of Army Staff Civil-Military Cooperation Project at Eruwa General Hospital.

The project, commissioned in Eruwa on Friday, comprises the remodelling and reconstruction of the hospital’s VIP/Physiotherapy Section, as well as the provision of a solar-powered borehole to strengthen water supply and support healthcare delivery at the facility.

Ogunlade described the initiative as a practical demonstration of how institutional partnerships can translate into tangible benefits for communities, particularly in areas where improved public infrastructure can directly affect the quality of life of residents.

He specially acknowledged the presence and contribution of Major General Oluremi A. Fadairo, the General Officer Commanding 82 Division, Nigerian Army, and Commander, Joint Task Force (South East), Operation UDO KA, who was in Eruwa for the commissioning. Major General Fadairo is an illustrious son of Eruwaland.

“Leadership must be about service, responsibility and results. Our people deserve representation that listens, collaborates and works deliberately to turn opportunities into sustainable development,” Ogunlade added.
