Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, through his media office, has felicitated with Nigerians on the occasion of the country’s 66th Independence Anniversary, calling for unity, peace, good governance and sustained development.

In a statement issued by BABS Media on Thursday, October 1, 2026, Ogunlade joined Nigerians at home and abroad in celebrating the nation’s independence while acknowledging the resilience, diversity and enduring aspirations of the Nigerian people.

The statement also conveyed prayers for the continued peace and prosperity of Nigeria, as well as wisdom and strength for the country’s leaders.

Ogunlade, who is the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for Ibarapa East State Constituency in the 2027 Oyo State House of Assembly election, expressed support for the continued implementation of the Federal Government’s Renewed Hope Agenda under President Bola Ahmed Tinubu.

At the local level, Ogunlade extended his Independence Day message to residents of Ibarapa East, including families, farmers, traders, artisans, youths, women, elders and other residents across Eruwa, Lanlate and surrounding communities.

“As we look towards the 2027 general elections, let us remain united, peaceful and committed to the vision of a new and better Ibarapa East and Oyo State,” the statement said.
