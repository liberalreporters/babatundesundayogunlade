Public Priorities

Policy areas for dialogue, advocacy and constructive representation across Ibarapa East.

Four Priority Areas

Building Opportunity and Wellbeing

These themes are a framework for public discussion and should be aligned with verified, feasible policy commitments.

♧
Priority 01

Agricultural Revitalisation & Farmer Support

Advocate for accessible agricultural inputs, stronger farmer cooperatives, extension services, storage and processing opportunities, and better farm-to-market connections.

  • Support cooperative and micro-enterprise access to finance
  • Promote value addition and local processing
  • Advocate for rural and feeder-road improvements
⌘
Priority 02

Youth Empowerment & Digital Skills

Promote practical skills, vocational learning, digital literacy and pathways to sustainable employment and enterprise for young people.

  • Skills and digital-economy training advocacy
  • Entrepreneurship and mentorship opportunities
  • Partnerships with training and industry providers
⚡
Priority 03

Infrastructure, Water & Clean Energy

Advance community-informed priorities for essential infrastructure, safe water access, energy reliability, sanitation and safer public spaces.

  • Advocate for water and sanitation projects
  • Support feasible clean-energy interventions
  • Promote maintenance and transparent project tracking
✚
Priority 04

Healthcare & Education

Encourage improved access to primary healthcare, learning resources, student support and inclusive community wellbeing initiatives.

  • Advocate for better-equipped health facilities
  • Promote education support and skills pathways
  • Encourage preventive health and outreach partnerships
